2. Defect-Based Refund Policy

Once a $249/year annual license is purchased and your license key is delivered, purchases and subscription renewals are generally final and non-refundable, except in the following verified cases:

  • Unresolved Material Technical Defect: If BIMAnvil suffers from a reproducible technical defect that prevents core advertised functionality from operating on a supported 64-bit Windows 10/11 system meeting our minimum requirements (8 GB RAM, OpenGL/WebGL GPU), and our support team (support@bimanvil.com) cannot resolve or provide a working fix within a reasonable troubleshooting window.
  • Duplicate / Accidental Double Charge: If an identical order was mistakenly charged twice due to a checkout or payment error.

Refunds are not issued simply due to change of mind after evaluation, lack of IFC authoring tools, unsupported operating systems (such as macOS or Linux without a Windows environment), or unused time after purchasing.

3. How to Request a Technical Refund

To submit a refund request based on a reproducible technical defect or billing duplicate:

  • Email support@bimanvil.com within 14 days of purchase (or within 14 days of encountering a newly introduced regression);
  • Include your Freemius Order ID / License Email, your Windows OS version and hardware summary, and clear reproduction steps or screenshots so our engineering team can troubleshoot;
  • Allow our support team a reasonable opportunity to resolve the defect. If the issue cannot be resolved, we will authorize a full refund through Freemius Inc. (our Merchant of Record) back to your original payment method, and the corresponding license key will be deactivated.

4. Subscription Renewal & Cancellation

Annual licenses renew automatically unless cancelled prior to the renewal date. You may cancel auto-renewal at any time with zero penalty through your Freemius Customer Portal (linked in your purchase receipt email) or by writing to support@bimanvil.com at least 24 hours before renewal. After cancellation, you continue to have full access through the remainder of your current paid annual term.
