2. License Keys, Invoices & Seat Transfers (Freemius)

  • Activate a License: After completing checkout via Freemius, check your email for your license key, then enter it inside BIMAnvil's activation dialog.
  • Move to a New Computer: Release your previous workstation seat from the Freemius Customer Portal (linked in your purchase receipt) or email support@bimanvil.com with your order email.
  • Invoices & Subscription Cancellation: Download VAT invoices or cancel auto-renewal directly inside your Freemius Customer Portal with zero penalty.

3. System Requirements

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB+ recommended for large federated IFC models)
  • Graphics: Modern dedicated or integrated GPU supporting OpenGL / WebGL
  • Supported Schemas: IFC2X3, IFC4, and IFC4X3

4. Reporting a Defect or Issue

To help us reproduce and resolve issues quickly, please include:

  • Your BIMAnvil version (e.g., v1.0.0 from GitHub Releases) and Windows version;
  • What action you were performing (e.g., rule ID, coordination test, or export format);
  • Screenshots or exact error details (please do not send confidential project IFC models unless anonymized or cleared by your organization).
