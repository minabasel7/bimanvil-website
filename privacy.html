Local-First Commitment: BIMAnvil parses, audits, and processes all IFC models, clash calculations, and takeoffs 100% locally on your Windows workstation. We never upload, store, or inspect your BIM models or project data on external servers.

1. Data Controller & Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how BIMAnvil Software ("BIMAnvil", operating from Egypt, contact: hello@bimanvil.com) handles information when you visit https://bimanvil.com, run the BIMAnvil desktop software, or purchase a license.

2. BIM & Project Data (Zero Cloud Uploads)

The BIMAnvil Windows application operates offline on your local computer hardware. Your .ifc models, geometry meshes, IDS files, schedules, cost data, and exported BCF/Excel files remain exclusively on your local storage. BIMAnvil does not collect, harvest, or sell model metadata or telemetry from your engineering projects.

3. License Activation & Update Checks

When you activate a commercial license key or check for updates, the desktop application connects over HTTPS only as needed to:

  • Validate and activate your license key with Freemius (machine/install identifier and license key verification);
  • Check the public release manifest for available software updates.

4. Payments and Personal Data (Merchant of Record — Freemius)

We use Freemius, Inc. as our Merchant of Record. When you make a purchase, personal data such as your name, email address, billing address/country, IP address (for currency, fraud prevention, and tax/VAT compliance), payment details, and transaction information is collected by or shared with Freemius and stored to process payments, manage subscriptions and licenses, and provide billing support.

BIMAnvil does not store raw credit card or banking details. Freemius shares order and customer contact details with us solely to enable license fulfillment, renewals, refund verification, and product support. For more information about Freemius's data practices and your privacy rights, see the Freemius Privacy Policy.

5. Website Visits & Support Communications

  • Marketing Website: Our static website loads styling, Google Fonts, and Lucide icon assets cleanly to render the page and does not embed third-party behavioral advertising trackers or cross-site ad cookies.
  • Hosting Logs: Standard edge web server logs (such as IP address and requested URL) may be temporarily processed by our hosting provider (Vercel) for reliability, DDoS protection, and security.
  • Support Emails: If you email support@bimanvil.com or hello@bimanvil.com, we keep your email address and message history solely to diagnose technical issues, manage seat transfers, or process support requests.

6. Data Sharing & Retention

We never sell or rent personal information. Data is shared only with essential infrastructure/payment processors required to deliver the product (primarily Freemius Inc. for order processing and licensing, and our hosting/email providers) or when legally required by valid authority. Billing records are retained as required for accounting and tax compliance.

7. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Users)

Regardless of your region (including EEA/UK GDPR and California CCPA users), you have the right to request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of personal support or account data we hold about you, as well as to opt out of optional communications. You can manage your billing details via the Freemius Customer Portal or contact hello@bimanvil.com.
